## The definition of "fully working"

For your website, I would consider it genuinely production-ready when this is true:

**Visitor → finds company → explores products → views technical information → selects product → submits RFQ → uploads drawing → PAKO receives enquiry → customer receives confirmation → PAKO can manage enquiry → website is fast/mobile/SEO-friendly → Google can index it.**

That's more important than simply having a polished frontend.

Your current website appears to have the **frontend structure and content architecture largely in place**. The next major focus should be the **real business functionality, especially RFQ/contact submission, email handling, file uploads, domain/SEO, and final production testing**.
